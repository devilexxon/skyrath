Skyrath Privacy Policy

Skyrath: Dragon Flight, by Sabbir Ahmed Shuvo (DevilExxon). Last updated: 19 November 2026 (optional rewarded ads added).

This policy explains what information Skyrath handles. The short version: the developer does not collect, sell or share any personal information. Your progress is saved on your phone and, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, also in your own Google account so you never lose it. Optional video ads (which you choose to watch for coins or gems) are provided by Google AdMob, which handles some device data as described below.

Android app (Google Play)

  • No analytics and no tracking by the developer. The developer does not run any server and does not receive your data.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device. Coins, gems, upgrades, skins, settings and mission progress are saved in the app's own storage on your phone.
  • Cloud save with Google Play Games (version 1.9.0 and later). If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy of that same game progress is stored in your Google account using Google Play Games Services (Saved Games), so it comes back after you reinstall the game or change phones. Google Play Games also shows your Play Games name (gamer name) in the game's settings. This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; the developer cannot see it. You can sign out of Play Games, or delete your Play Games data, in the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.
  • Optional rewarded ads (version 1.10.1 and later). You can choose to watch a short video ad in exchange for coins or gems. Nothing is shown unless you tap a "Watch an ad" button. These ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from the IP address), and information about how ads are shown and used, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice first, and you can change it any time in Settings, "Ad privacy". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Network use. The game itself works offline. The internet permission lets Android's built-in web engine run the game lets Google Play Games sync your cloud save, and lets Google AdMob load the optional ads. The game does not contact any other server.
  • No permissions for your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files are requested.

Web versions

When you play Skyrath on a game website, that website's own privacy policy also applies. On CrazyGames, the game uses the CrazyGames SDK to save your progress to your CrazyGames account and to show optional ads; see the CrazyGames privacy policy. On itch.io, progress is saved only in your browser; see the itch.io privacy policy.

Children

Skyrath is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example ads or online saves), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/devilexxon/skyrath/issues, or use the developer contact shown on the game's Google Play page.
