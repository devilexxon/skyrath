Skyrath Privacy Policy

Skyrath: Dragon Flight, by Sabbir Ahmed Shuvo (DevilExxon). Last updated: 18 November 2026.

This policy explains what information Skyrath handles. The short version: the Android app does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

Android app (Google Play)

  • No personal data is collected. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your game progress stays on your device. Coins, gems, upgrades, settings and mission progress are saved in the app's own local storage on your phone. They never leave the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
  • No network use. The whole game is inside the app and works offline. The app's internet permission is only there because Android's built-in web engine, which runs the game, needs it to start. The game does not contact any server.
  • No permissions for your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files are requested.

Web versions

When you play Skyrath on a game website, that website's own privacy policy also applies. On CrazyGames, the game uses the CrazyGames SDK to save your progress to your CrazyGames account and to show optional ads; see the CrazyGames privacy policy. On itch.io, progress is saved only in your browser; see the itch.io privacy policy.

Children

Skyrath is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example ads or online saves), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/devilexxon/skyrath/issues, or use the developer contact shown on the game's Google Play page.
